About The Thinking Cell

The Thinking Cell was created to explore complex biological mechanisms behind debilitating diseases, understanding how exactly therapeutics behind a drug works, and also to create a space for supporting the mental and emotional challenges faced by researchers in STEM.



Meet The Team
