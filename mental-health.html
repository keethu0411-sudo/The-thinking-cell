Mental Health in STEM

Science can be demanding, isolating, and emotionally exhausting. This space exists to remind students, researchers, and aspiring scientists that they are not alone.


We hope to build a community where people in STEM can feel supported, heard, and understood.


If you are struggling significantly, please seek professional support or local mental health services.
